The Problem

The Questions Your Content Never Answers

AI assistants rarely search a buyer's question exactly as it was typed. They split it into smaller questions on cost, security, integrations and alternatives, then cite whichever pages answer each one cleanly.

Most of those questions never show up in a keyword tool, so content teams end up guessing, and the citation goes to a competitor who answered what nobody on your team knew was being asked.

Asked takes a keyword and returns the questions AI search engines are likely to ask about it, labels each one with the evidence behind it, and turns your selection into a content brief.

Keyword tools rank phrases by search volume, while Asked plans for the sub-questions AI search runs behind a query, many of which show no volume at all. It also shows the evidence behind every question instead of a bare list.
